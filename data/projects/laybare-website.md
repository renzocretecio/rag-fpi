# Project Context Document: Lay Bare Brand & Services Website

## Executive Overview
- **Project Name:** Lay Bare Official Website & Brand Platform
- **Project Type:** Production Single-Page Application (SPA)
- **Industry/Domain:** Beauty, Personal Care & Franchise Retail
- **Developer Role:** Lead Front-End Developer
- **Primary Tech Stack:** Vue.js, Vuex, Tailwind CSS, Custom REST APIs

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Operational Bottlenecks
- **Absence of Web Presence:** Lay Bare lacked a modern, central web application to showcase their brands and comprehensive service offerings to the public.
- **Service & Branch Discovery:** Customers had difficulty finding verified branch details, product lineups, and service packages online.
- **Zero Marketing Telemetry:** The marketing team had no data analytics or metrics to evaluate customer brand interest, search patterns, or campaign effectiveness.

---

## 2. Solutions & Technical Architecture

### A. Frontend Architecture & State Management
- **Framework & SPA Setup:** Built a production-grade single-page application completely from scratch using Vue.js and Vuex for centralized state management.
- **Responsive UI/UX:** Transformed high-fidelity layouts from marketing into responsive, pixel-perfect pages using Tailwind CSS.
- **Performance Optimization:** Optimized asset delivery and component rendering to ensure rapid page-load times and smooth client navigation.

### B. Analytics & Data Telemetry
- **Analytics Integration:** Embedded Google Analytics and structured event tracking across key user interaction points.
- **SEO Optimization:** Implemented technical search engine optimization strategies to drive organic discovery and brand search volume.

### C. Backend API Integration
- **Branch & Service APIs:** Developed and connected custom API services to dynamically deliver branch locations, service options, and product data to the frontend.

---

## 3. Measurable Outcomes & Key Business Impact
- **Enhanced Brand Visibility:** Provided a sleek, accessible digital platform for customers to explore services and locate branches effortlessly.
- **Data-Backed Marketing:** Equipped marketing leads with real-time analytics to monitor key page views, direct web traffic, and social outbound clicks.
- **Search Growth:** Measurably increased organic brand search volume through targeted technical SEO improvements.
